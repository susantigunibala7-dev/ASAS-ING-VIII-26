      Kunci Jawaban, Bobot & Pembahasan Lengkap

      Naskah ASAS Bahasa Inggris Kelas VIII Semester Ganjil (25 Butir Soal)

      No. 1 • Tipe: PG (Bobot 1 Poin) Kunci: A (can)
      Aisyah is very good at singing. She _____ sing traditional Islamic songs beautifully.
      Pembahasan: Kalimat pertama menyatakan kemampuan ("very good at singing"), sehingga modal verb yang tepat untuk menyatakan kemampuan (ability) adalah "can".
      No. 2 • Tipe: PG (Bobot 1 Poin) Kunci: A (can)
      Complete the dialogue above with the most appropriate word!
      Pembahasan: Fatimah menawarkan bantuan (kesanggupan/kemampuan membantu di waktu sekarang), sehingga di antara pilihan yang tersedia kata yang tepat adalah "can".
      No. 3 • Tipe: PG (Bobot 1 Poin) Kunci: C (I will help my teacher clean the classroom.)
      Which sentence expresses willingness?
      Pembahasan: Modal verb "will" digunakan untuk menyatakan kemauan/kesediaan (willingness) melakukan sesuatu secara sukarela: "I will help my teacher clean the classroom."
      No. 4 • Tipe: PG (Bobot 1 Poin) Kunci: C (are studying)
      Look! The students _____ English in the classroom right now.
      Pembahasan: Penanda waktu "Look!" dan "right now" menunjukkan Present Continuous Tense (S + am/is/are + V-ing). Subjek jamak "The students" menggunakan "are studying".
      No. 5 • Tipe: PG (Bobot 1 Poin) Kunci: B (is cooking)
      My mother _____ dinner in the kitchen right now.
      Pembahasan: Subjek tunggal "My mother" dengan keterangan waktu "right now" menggunakan Present Continuous Tense: "is cooking".
      No. 6 • Tipe: PG (Bobot 1 Poin) Kunci: A (is taking)
      "Budi usually rides his bicycle to school, but today he _____ the bus because it is raining."
      Pembahasan: Kejadian sementara yang sedang berlangsung hari ini ("today ... because it is raining") dengan subjek tunggal "he" menggunakan "is taking".
      No. 7 • Tipe: PG (Bobot 1 Poin) Kunci: C (They are playing football.)
      Which sentence is grammatically correct?
      Pembahasan: Struktur Present Continuous Tense yang benar untuk subjek jamak "They" adalah Subject + are + Verb-ing ("They are playing football.").
      No. 8 • Tipe: PG (Bobot 1 Poin) Kunci: A (My father was sleeping when the phone rang.)
      Arrange the jumbled words above into a meaningful and grammatically correct sentence!
      Pembahasan: Susunan Past Continuous Tense yang disela kejadian Simple Past adalah: "My father was sleeping when the phone rang."
      No. 9 • Tipe: PG (Bobot 1 Poin) Kunci: B (when)
      Rina was reading an English book _____ her brother came into the room.
      Pembahasan: Kata hubung "when" digunakan sebelum klausa Simple Past ("her brother came into the room") yang menyela aktivitas yang sedang berlangsung (Past Continuous).
      No. 10 • Tipe: PG (Bobot 1 Poin) Kunci: D (She was not reading a novel.)
      Which sentence correctly uses Past Continuous Tense in negative form?
      Pembahasan: Bentuk negatif Past Continuous Tense untuk subjek tunggal "She" menggunakan "was not + V-ing": "She was not reading a novel."
      No. 11 • Tipe: PG (Bobot 1 Poin) Kunci: B (He can play the guitar.)
      What can Raka do?
      Pembahasan: Pada teks tertulis jelas: "Raka can play the guitar very well because he practices every afternoon."
      No. 12 • Tipe: PG (Bobot 1 Poin) Kunci: C (Play a musical instrument)
      What can Sinta NOT do?
      Pembahasan: Pada teks disebutkan: "Sinta can sing beautiful songs, but she cannot play any musical instruments."
      No. 13 • Tipe: PG (Bobot 1 Poin) Kunci: C (He can dance and play the traditional drum.)
      What can Budi do according to the story?
      Pembahasan: Pada kalimat terakhir teks tertulis: "Meanwhile, Budi can dance traditional dances and can also play the traditional drum."
      No. 14 • Tipe: PG (Bobot 1 Poin) Kunci: B (Bought)
      The correct answer to fill in the blank (14) is …
      Pembahasan: Peristiwa terjadi akhir pekan lalu ("last weekend" - Simple Past Tense), sehingga bentuk Verb-2 dari "buy" adalah "bought".
      No. 15 • Tipe: PG (Bobot 1 Poin) Kunci: D (I didn’t)
      The correct answer to fill in the blank (15) is …
      Pembahasan: Pertanyaan "Did you go there with your sister?" dijawab negatif ("No"), maka jawaban singkat Simple Past Tense adalah "I didn’t".
      No. 16 • Tipe: BS (Bobot 6 Poin) Kunci Tabel: T - F - F
      Read each statement carefully. Choose TRUE (T) if the statement is correct and FALSE (F) if it is incorrect according to the picture!
      • Mike is cooking in the kitchen. [T]
      • Mia and Jack are standing on the chair. [F]
      • Charlie are playing tennis. [F]
      Pembahasan: 1) TRUE: Mike terlihat sedang memasak di dapur. 2) FALSE: Mia dan Jack duduk di kursi belajar, bukan berdiri di atas kursi. 3) FALSE: Secara tata bahasa salah ("Charlie are" seharusnya "Charlie is") dan mereka bermain tenis meja (table tennis).
      No. 17 • Tipe: BS (Bobot 6 Poin) Kunci Tabel: F - F - F
      Based on the picture above, choose TRUE (T) or FALSE (F) for each statement!
      • Jake was watching TV at 11:15 a.m. [F]
      • Jake was playing with the cat at 7:45 p.m. [F]
      • Jake Father was cooking at 9:00 a.m. [F]
      Pembahasan: 1) FALSE: Pukul 11:15 a.m. Jake bermain game komputer ("play computer games"), bukan menonton TV. 2) FALSE: Pukul 7:45 p.m. Jake bermain gitar ("play a guitar"), bukan bermain dengan kucing. 3) FALSE: Pada jadwal, ayah Jake memasak sarapan pukul 8:00 a.m., bukan 9:00 a.m.
      No. 18 • Tipe: BS (Bobot 6 Poin) Kunci Tabel: F - T - F
      Choose TRUE (T) if the statement matches the story and FALSE (F) if it does not!
      • Arlene Kelsch was sitting on her front veranda when she saw something moving in the water. [F]
      • At first, Arlene thought the animals were snakes, but they were actually two alligators. [T]
      • The Gator Squad told Arlene to go outside and catch the alligators herself. [F]
      Pembahasan: 1) FALSE: Arlene duduk di beranda belakang ("back veranda"), bukan depan. 2) TRUE: Awalnya ia mengira ular, padahal dua ekor aligator. 3) FALSE: Gator Squad menyuruhnya tetap berada di dalam rumah ("stay inside her house").
      No. 19 • Tipe: BS (Bobot 6 Poin) Kunci Tabel: T - F - T
      The following statements are Present Continuous Tense statements. Choose TRUE (T) if the sentence is grammatically correct and FALSE (F) if it is grammatically incorrect.
      • She is studying English in the classroom. [T]
      • They are plays football in the school yard. [F]
      • The students are doing their homework. [T]
      Pembahasan: 1) TRUE: She + is + studying. 2) FALSE: Setelah "are" harus diikuti V-ing ("playing"), bukan "plays". 3) TRUE: The students + are + doing.
      No. 20 • Tipe: BS (Bobot 6 Poin) Kunci Tabel: F - F - T
      The following statements are Past Continuous Tense statements. Choose TRUE (T) if the sentence is grammatically correct and FALSE (F) if it is grammatically incorrect.
      • The students was doing their homework when the teacher arrived. [F]
      • My father were reading a newspaper yesterday morning. [F]
      • While Rina was reading a book, her brother was drawing a picture. [T]
      Pembahasan: 1) FALSE: Subjek jamak "The students" harus menggunakan "were", bukan "was". 2) FALSE: Subjek tunggal "My father" harus menggunakan "was", bukan "were". 3) TRUE: Dua kejadian berlangsung bersamaan di masa lampau ("While Rina was reading..., her brother was drawing...").
      No. 21 • Tipe: URAIAN (Bobot 4.2 Poin)

      Change the following sentences into negative (-) and interrogative (?) forms!

      1. She can speak English.
      2. They will help their teacher.
      Pedoman Jawaban Uraian:
      a. (-) She cannot (can't) speak English. (?) Can she speak English? b. (-) They will not (won't) help their teacher. (?) Will they help their teacher?
      Pembahasan: Bentuk negatif menambahkan "not" setelah modal (cannot / will not), sedangkan bentuk tanya meletakkan modal di awal kalimat (Can she...? / Will they...?).
      No. 22 • Tipe: URAIAN (Bobot 4.2 Poin)

      Complete the sentences using the correct form of Present Continuous Tense!

      1. Aisyah _____ (read) an English book right now.
      2. The students _____ (practice) speaking English at the moment.
      Pedoman Jawaban Uraian:
      a. is reading b. are practicing
      Pembahasan: Rumus Present Continuous: S + am/is/are + V-ing. "Aisyah" (tunggal) -> is reading; "The students" (jamak) -> are practicing.
      No. 23 • Tipe: URAIAN (Bobot 4.2 Poin)

      What are the past form (V2) of these verbs?

      a. Meet : __________
      c. Accept : __________
      b. Give : __________
      d. Think : __________
      Pedoman Jawaban Uraian:
      a. Met | b. Gave | c. Accepted | d. Thought
      Pembahasan: Meet -> Met (irregular), Give -> Gave (irregular), Accept -> Accepted (regular), Think -> Thought (irregular).
      No. 24 • Tipe: URAIAN (Bobot 4.2 Poin)

      Change the following sentences into Simple Past Tense!

      1. Ahmad goes to the mosque every morning.
      2. We eat breakfast together.
      Pedoman Jawaban Uraian:
      a. Ahmad went to the mosque yesterday morning (every morning). b. We ate breakfast together.
      Pembahasan: Ubah kata kerja ke bentuk lampau (V2): "goes" menjadi "went", dan "eat" menjadi "ate".
      No. 25 • Tipe: URAIAN (Bobot 4.2 Poin)

      Answer these questions based on the text above:

      1. Where did Farhan go last Saturday?
      2. What did Farhan do at his grandfather's house?
      3. What was his grandfather doing while Farhan was watering the plants?
      Pedoman Jawaban Uraian:
      a. He went to his grandfather's house. b. He helped his grandfather clean the garden and watered the plants. c. His grandfather was preparing some food in the kitchen.
      Pembahasan: Semua jawaban tersurat langsung pada paragraf bacaan menggunakan bentuk lampau (Simple Past & Past Continuous).

      Pengaturan Akses & Keamanan CBT

      Jika diisi, nilai siswa akan otomatis dikirim ke Google Sheets saat ujian selesai maupun saat diskualifikasi.

      ☁️ Panduan Integrasi Google Sheets (Code.gs)

      1. Buka Google Sheets baru ➔ klik menu Ekstensi > Apps Script.
      2. Tempel kode lengkap dari file Code.gs (atau cuplikan di bawah), lalu klik Deploy > New Deployment > Web App (Execute as: Me, Who has access: Anyone).
      3. Salin URL Web App yang muncul dan tempelkan ke kolom URL Webhook di samping.
      function doPost(e) {
  var lock = LockService.getScriptLock();
  lock.waitLock(15000);
  try {
    var ss = SpreadsheetApp.getActiveSpreadsheet();
    var sheet = ss.getSheetByName("REKAP_UTAMA") || ss.getActiveSheet();
    if (sheet.getLastRow() === 0) {
      sheet.appendRow(["No","Timestamp","Kode","Nama","Kelas","Mapel","WaktuSelesai","Durasi","PGBenar","SkorPG(15)","BSBenar","SkorBS(30)","Uraian","SkorUraian(21)","TotalSkor(66)","NilaiAkhir","Status","Pelanggaran"]);
    }
    var d = JSON.parse(e.postData.contents);
    var r = sheet.getLastRow() + 1;
    var totFormula = d.disqualified ? 0 : "=IF(ISNUMBER(N" + r + "), J" + r + "+L" + r + "+N" + r + ", J" + r + "+L" + r + ")";
    var valFormula = d.disqualified ? 0 : "=IF(Q" + r + "=\"DISKUALIFIKASI\", 0, ROUND((O" + r + "/66)*100, 1))";
    sheet.appendRow([
      r - 1, new Date(), d.id, d.name, d.className, d.subject,
      d.finishedAt, d.durationUsed, d.pgCorrect + "/" + d.pgTotalQuestions, d.pgScore,
      d.bsStatementsCorrect + "/" + d.bsStatementsTotal, d.bsScore,
      d.essayMarkedDone + "/" + d.essayTotal, "", totFormula, valFormula,
      d.disqualified ? "DISKUALIFIKASI" : "SELESAI", d.violations
    ]);
    return ContentService.createTextOutput(JSON.stringify({status: "success"}))
      .setMimeType(ContentService.MimeType.JSON);
  } finally {
    lock.releaseLock();
  }
}
